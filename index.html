OISA · 2026
9th edition — Oukaimeden International School for Astrophysics

From JWST to Radio Astronomy: Modern Views of the Cosmos

A week of advanced astrophysics training, spanning lecture halls in Marrakech and the dome of the Oukaimeden Observatory, one of Africa's leading astronomical facilities.

14 – 19 December 2026 · Marrakech & Oukaimeden, Morocco

At a glance

An international advanced-training school, hosted by Cadi Ayyad University.

OISA builds on eight previous editions that established the school as a recognised scientific event, combining high-level lectures, hands-on workshops and direct interaction with international researchers.

Designed for Master's students, PhD candidates and early-career researchers, the 2026 edition gives particular consideration to applicants from African institutions, while maintaining strong international participation. The working language is English.

Seven scientific themes

The programme spans the major areas of contemporary astrophysics.

Galaxy formation and evolution with JWST

Recent observations and new constraints on the earliest galaxies.

Exoplanets and planetary systems

Detection methods and characterisation of worlds beyond the Solar System.

Stellar physics and variable stars

Stellar life cycles and the observational signatures of their variability.

Extragalactic astronomy

Large-scale structure and distant galaxy populations.

Space sciences and space weather

Sun–Earth interactions and their impact on space-based technology.

Radio astronomy and multi-wavelength astrophysics

Combined approaches across the electromagnetic spectrum.

Small bodies of the Solar System

Comets, asteroids and meteors as witnesses to planet formation.

OISA contributes to the development of astronomy and space sciences in Africa by providing high-level training, fostering international cooperation, and giving access to modern observational facilities and scientific networks.

Week overview

Each thematic day pairs advanced lectures with hands-on workshops and time for discussion with speakers.

Thematic days

Advanced lectures delivered by international experts, data-analysis workshops, scientific discussions and networking time, spread across the programme's seven themes.

  • Lectures in the morning
  • Practical workshops and data analysis in the afternoon
  • Q&A sessions at the end of each day
Dec 19

Scientific visit to one of Africa's leading astronomical facilities, followed by the closing session.

  • Registration fees are fully waived
  • Accommodation and meals are fully covered throughout the school, in both Marrakech and Oukaimeden
